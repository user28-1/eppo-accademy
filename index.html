What is EPPO?

The European Public Prosecutor’s Office (EPPO) is the independent prosecution office of the European Union responsible for investigating, prosecuting and bringing to judgment criminal offences affecting the financial interests of the European Union.

EPPO Competence Map

EPPO Competence

  • EU Funds Fraud
  • Corruption affecting EU finances
  • Misappropriation of EU funds
  • Money laundering linked to PIF offences
  • EU Customs Duties Fraud
  • Cross-border VAT Fraud
  • Criminal Organizations targeting EU funds

Not EPPO Competence

  • Murder
  • Robbery
  • Terrorism
  • Drug trafficking
  • Ordinary national tax offences
  • Excise evasion (simple cases)
